About Human Agency

Built by fans, run like an agency.

Anime, webtoons and comics have become global culture — but the people who make and carry that culture are often unrepresented. Human Agency exists to change that: we sign, protect and grow creative talent, from the illustrator drawing tonight's chapter to the voice actor, cosplayer, streamer or celebrity bringing it to life.

Our values

  • Creators first. Fair deals, transparent splits, creator-owned IP.
  • Fandom fluent. We know the culture because we live in it.
  • Global by default. Japan, Korea, the Americas, Europe and SEA.
