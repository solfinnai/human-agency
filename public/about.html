About Human Agency

The talent agency for fandom.

Anime, webtoons, comics, cosplay and VTubing are global culture, yet most of the people who make that culture have no representation. Big agencies chase mainstream celebrities, and platforms don't advocate for individual creators. Human Agency represents the people behind the panels and the stars around them, and turns their audiences and IP into lasting careers.

What we stand for

  • You keep your IP. We license and grow your work. We never take ownership of it.
  • Transparent payouts. Clear statements and on-time payments, every time.
  • Fandom-native. We build brand packages that feel at home in the community.

Where we're going

We're starting with five fandom desks and building toward every niche where audiences gather, from gaming and music to film, fitness and travel. Screen deals are handled through a licensed partner agency.
